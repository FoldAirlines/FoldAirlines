Flight manual

read before takeoff!

  • One thumb, full control

    Push the joystick and the plane glides up, down, left or right. Let go and it levels out.

  • Thread the gaps

    Doorways, holes in walls, gaps between shelves. If there is a way through, you can fit.

  • Fly close, score big

    Every near miss adds to your combo. The closer you pass, the faster the points stack up.

  • Collect gold

    Grab the stars and fly through the gold rings. Spend it in the hangar on new planes.

  • The house never ends

    Library, kitchen, classroom, rooftops and more. The rooms keep coming and the plane keeps speeding up.

  • Pick your view

    Three camera distances. Stay close for speed or pull back to see what is coming.
